What Is Carpet Area?

Carpet area is generally the usable floor area within the internal walls of a property. It is an important measurement when comparing residential properties because quoted areas can otherwise be difficult to compare.

Built-up and saleable or super built-up areas can include additional areas depending on the property's specifications and the way a developer presents the measurement.

The loading percentage used here is an estimate for comparison purposes. Always verify the official carpet area and other area measurements in the agreement, sanctioned plan and project documents.

Why Carpet Area Matters in Mumbai

Two Mumbai apartments can have similar quoted saleable areas but different usable carpet areas. Comparing the carpet area can help buyers understand how much usable space they are actually getting.

For investment analysis, comparing the total purchase price against carpet area can also provide a more consistent price-per-square-foot metric.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is carpet area?

Carpet area refers to the usable floor area within the internal walls of a property, subject to the applicable legal definition.

What is loading?

Loading is a commonly used real-estate term for the difference between carpet area and a larger quoted area such as built-up or saleable area. The exact components can vary by project.

Is built-up area the same as carpet area?

No. Built-up area is generally larger than carpet area because it can include wall areas and other components depending on the measurement method.

How do I compare property prices using carpet area?

Divide the total property price by the carpet area to calculate the effective price per square foot on a carpet-area basis.

Should I rely only on this calculator when buying a property?

No. Use the calculator as an estimate and verify the official area measurements and property documents before making a purchase decision.

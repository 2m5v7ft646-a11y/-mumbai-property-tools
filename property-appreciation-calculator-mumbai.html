How Property Appreciation Is Calculated

Property appreciation refers to the increase in the market value of a property over time.

This calculator uses compound annual growth. For example, a property that appreciates by 6% each year is assumed to grow by 6% on its value from the previous year.

The actual value of a Mumbai property can be influenced by location, infrastructure, supply and demand, redevelopment potential, rental demand, market conditions and the specific project or building.

The result is an estimate based entirely on the assumptions you enter. It does not guarantee future property prices or investment returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is property appreciation?

Property appreciation is the increase in a property's market value over time.

How is future property value calculated?

This calculator applies compound annual growth to the current property value using the annual appreciation rate you enter.

What is a reasonable property appreciation rate in Mumbai?

There is no single rate that applies to all Mumbai properties. Appreciation can vary significantly by locality, project, property type and market cycle. Use different assumptions to compare scenarios rather than relying on a guaranteed rate.

Does rental income count as property appreciation?

No. Property appreciation measures the increase in the property's value. Rental income is a separate component of an investment's total return.

Is property appreciation guaranteed?

No. Property values can rise or fall depending on market conditions and the characteristics of the property.

How Property ROI Is Calculated

Property ROI measures the return generated by an investment compared with the total money invested.

A realistic property investment calculation should consider more than just the difference between the purchase and selling price. Stamp duty, registration, renovation expenses, rental income, brokerage and other costs can materially affect the actual return.

This calculator provides an estimate for planning and comparison. Actual returns will depend on the property's performance and the costs associated with the transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is property ROI?

Property ROI is the return generated from a property investment relative to the total money invested.

Does rental income increase property ROI?

Yes. Rental income can contribute to the overall return from a property investment and should be considered alongside capital appreciation.

Should stamp duty be included when calculating property ROI?

Yes. Including stamp duty, registration and other acquisition costs can provide a more realistic picture of the investment's return.

Does this calculator include income tax or capital gains tax?

No. Tax treatment can vary depending on the investor, property and transaction. Consult a qualified tax professional for tax-specific calculations.

Is property ROI guaranteed?

No. This calculator provides an estimate based on the assumptions you enter. Actual property values, rent, costs and selling prices can differ significantly.

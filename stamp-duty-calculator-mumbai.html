How Mumbai Stamp Duty Is Calculated

For a property transaction, stamp duty is generally calculated using the applicable market value / Ready Reckoner valuation. If the agreement value is lower than the applicable valuation, the higher value may be relevant for calculating duty.

For planning purposes, this calculator currently uses:

  • 6% for standard buyers in Mumbai.
  • 5% for eligible female-only buyers.
  • Registration fee: 1%, subject to the applicable maximum of ₹30,000 for properties above ₹30 lakh.

These figures are intended as an estimate and should not be treated as a legal or official quotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is stamp duty in Mumbai in 2026?

For planning purposes, Mumbai property transactions are commonly calculated at 6% for standard buyers and 5% for eligible female-only buyers. The exact applicable duty should be verified before registration.

Is stamp duty calculated on the agreement value?

The applicable valuation can depend on the agreement value and the government's Ready Reckoner / market valuation. Buyers should check the applicable valuation for their specific property.

How much is property registration in Mumbai?

Registration charges are generally calculated at 1%, subject to the applicable cap. For properties above ₹30 lakh, the commonly reported maximum is ₹30,000.

Do women get a stamp duty concession in Mumbai?

Eligible female-only purchasers can receive a lower stamp duty rate. The exact eligibility and applicable concession should be verified with the Maharashtra Registration & Stamps Department.

Does this calculator include GST?

No. GST, if applicable to an under-construction property, is separate from stamp duty and registration charges.

Important Before You Register

Use this calculator to estimate your budget, but confirm the final charges before signing or registering the agreement. Government rates, valuation rules, concessions and other applicable charges can change.

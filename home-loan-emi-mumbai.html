How Home Loan EMI Is Calculated

Home loan EMI depends mainly on three factors: the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure.

The EMI calculation uses the standard reducing-balance loan formula. A longer tenure generally reduces the monthly EMI but can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

This calculator is useful when comparing different Mumbai property budgets and deciding how much home loan you may be comfortable taking.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much EMI will I pay on a ₹50 lakh home loan?

The EMI depends on the interest rate and tenure. Enter ₹50,00,000, your expected interest rate and loan tenure above to calculate it.

Does a longer home loan tenure reduce EMI?

Generally, yes. Increasing the tenure spreads repayment over more months, which can reduce the monthly EMI. However, the total interest paid can become higher.

Should I take a higher loan amount to buy a Mumbai property?

The right loan amount depends on your income, existing obligations, down payment and comfort with the monthly EMI. Use this calculator to compare different loan scenarios before making a decision.

Does this calculator include processing fees?

No. This calculator estimates principal and interest repayment only. Bank processing fees, insurance, legal charges and other transaction costs are separate.

Can I use this calculator for an under-construction property?

Yes. You can use it to estimate the repayment for the home loan amount you expect to borrow. Actual disbursement and pre-EMI arrangements may differ depending on the lender and project.
